Compare Destination Costs

Travel costs can vary significantly depending on where you go. Comparing common expenses can help you choose a destination that fits your budget.

Typical Mid-Range Travel Costs Per Person

The table below compares typical mid-range travel expenses reported by travelers for several popular U.S. destinations. Actual costs can vary depending on travel style and season.

Destination Accommodation Food Local Transportation Entertainment
San Diego $124 $84 $23 $57
New York City $175 $87 $49 $180
Las Vegas $158 $139 $53 $78
Orlando $154 $113 $66 $204

Source: Budget Your Trip

Keep in Mind

These figures represent typical mid-range traveler spending and should be used as planning estimates rather than guaranteed prices. Costs can change depending on hotel choices, activities, trip dates, and personal spending habits.
