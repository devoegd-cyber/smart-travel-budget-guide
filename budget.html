Travel Budget Planner

Planning your expenses before a trip can help you avoid overspending and make the most of your travel budget.

Start With Your Total Budget

Decide how much you are comfortable spending on your entire trip. From there, divide your money between the major travel expenses such as transportation, lodging, food, activities, and emergency expenses.

Transportation

Include flights, rental cars, public transportation, rideshares, gas, and parking.

Lodging

Compare hotels and other accommodations to find an option that fits your budget and location.

Food

Estimate how much you expect to spend each day on meals, snacks, coffee, and drinks.

Activities

Set aside money for attractions, tours, entertainment, shopping, and other activities.

Budget Tip

Leave some extra room in your budget for unexpected costs. Having a small emergency fund can prevent an unplanned expense from affecting the rest of your trip.
